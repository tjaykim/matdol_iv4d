matdol — Privacy Policy

Summary: matdol does not collect, store or send any personal information.

1. What matdol does with your data
   - Image files you open are read only on your own computer, for display.
     They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
   - matdol does not use the network. It has no accounts, analytics,
     telemetry, advertising, crash reporting or automatic update checks.
   - matdol keeps a few display preferences (for example, whether the
     toolbars are pinned and the camera raw mode) in a small settings file
     in your user folder (matdol/settings.json). It contains no file names,
     file history or personal information, and never leaves your computer.
     Uninstalling matdol may leave this file; you can delete it at any time.

2. Clipboard and saved captures
   Captures (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+Shift+C) are placed on your system clipboard or
   saved to a file that you choose, only when you ask for it.

3. Donations and external links
   If you choose to donate, or open a link from matdol, your web browser opens
   an external website (for example a payment service). That website is
   operated by a third party and its own privacy policy applies. matdol itself
   receives no information about it.

4. App stores
   If you obtained matdol from an app store, the store may collect information
   under its own privacy policy (for example, installation statistics). The
   author may see aggregated statistics provided by the store, but not
   information that identifies you.

5. Children
   matdol does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
   children.

6. Changes
   If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be
   updated and shipped with that version.
